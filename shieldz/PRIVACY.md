## Privacy Policy

### Data collection

This plugin needs no credentials, stores nothing and collects no personal data.

It sends only the parameters of the tool you call:

- **Get cross-chain swap quote**: the two asset ids and the amount.
- **List swappable assets**: the optional symbol and chain filter.

### Third-party services

Requests go over HTTPS to Shieldz Swap, which runs the quote API: https://swap.shieldz.cash (privacy policy: https://swap.shieldz.cash/privacy). Every tool is read-only: it reads prices and routes and never moves funds or touches a wallet.
